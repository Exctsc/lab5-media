CCT360 Lab 5: Design with Sound and Vision

Erindale After Dark

An evening walk through my neighbourhood in Mississauga, from the Credit River up to the Erindale GO tracks. Listen first, then watch the street react.

Part A: Soundscape

80 seconds, in five parts. Headphones make it better.

  1. 0:00 The Credit River: water, wind, Canada geese flying over
  2. 0:14 Riverwood: footsteps on dry November leaves
  3. 0:28 Mississauga Road: cars, a bus stops, air brakes, door chime
  4. 0:46 The Crosswalk: locator ticks, then the walk signal
  5. 0:58 Erindale GO: crossing bell, train horn, the train going by

Part B: Visualscape

The scene follows the soundscape as it plays. Move your mouse over it, and click the geese, the bus, the train or the walk signal.
